origin `https://127.0.0.1:4330` · **Method:** the real application started with `app:run confirm:rehearsal`,
driven in headless Chromium over the normally trusted rehearsal CA, with a real staff MFA sign-in and a real

Captures and the machine-readable index are local-only under `.local/ui-audit/before/` (untracked; they

## What a viewer actually meets

Every screen renders, every control is wired to the canonical contract client, and the truthfulness
discipline is real: acknowledgement is never drawn as verification, unknown effects keep their own tone, and
the capability register refuses to overclaim. That engineering is the asset. The interface does not show it.

### The seven findings that matter

**A-01 — There is no product story.** Nothing in the interface states the sequence purpose → consent →
policy → workflow → system action → independent verification → evidence → test. A viewer has to
reconstruct ORVIA's thesis from eleven unrelated navigation items.

**A-02 — Identifiers dominate meaning.** The API returns `purpose_id`, `system_id`, `resource_id`; the UI
renders them raw. `list_purposes`, `list_systems` and `list_principals` already carry the names and are
never joined against. Control map, workflow list, mappings and action scope are all readable data rendered
unreadably.

**A-03 — The strongest idea is invisible.** "What ORVIA requested and what ORVIA independently observed are
different facts" is enforced in `derive.ts` and never *said*. On the workflow screen, execution receipts and
observation records are two `<h4>`s in the same card.

**A-04 — The Overview leads with the wrong thing.** Workflow counts are correct and meaningless out of
context; 252 "needing attention" with no framing reads as failure rather than as honest surfacing.

**A-05 — Unresolved work is undiagnosable.** Failure Centre cards carry no purpose, system, age or route to
the owning workflow except a sequential scan (up to 263 workflow reads at current fixture size), which is
unusable live.

**A-06 — The fixture is test debris, not a demonstration.** 323 purposes (313 `promotional_marketing`), 317
systems named `SYNTHETIC_CRM` / `Browser CRM 66c65afa`, 38 principals mostly called "Synthetic API-created
principal", 386 policies, 263 workflows, 252 unresolved obligations, **0 of 315 control-map edges carrying
any observation**. No amount of styling makes this read as a product. The demonstration needs one named,
intentional purpose it can point at.

**A-07 — A live demonstration cannot survive a pause.** `scripts/app-run.ts` held its advisory-lock
connection idle; `infrastructure/loopback.mjs` destroys any relayed connection idle for five minutes, so the
supervisor lost its lock and took web, worker and agent down with it. Reproduced during this audit: the
application exited with `Connection terminated unexpectedly` mid-session. The Playwright harness already
carries a 30-second heartbeat for exactly this; the supervisor did not.

### Browser health at audit time

7 console entries across 22 page loads, **all** of the form `Failed to load resource: … 401` on signed-out
pages and one deliberate `404`. Zero uncaught page errors, zero React correctness warnings, zero requests to
a foreign origin. The existing engineering quality bar holds; the problem is exclusively presentational —
plus A-06 and A-07, which are data and operations.

## What must not change

- Acknowledgement must never be drawn as verification.
- `EFFECT_UNKNOWN`, `MANUAL_REQUIRED`, expired and stale observations keep distinct, non-success treatment.
- No invented percentage, trend, score, stage or count.
- Technical identifiers stay available; they move, they are never removed.
- `NOT_RUN` canonical acceptance, prototype scope and the synthetic label stay visible.
