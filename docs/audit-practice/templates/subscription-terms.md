## 1. Licence

- Cyberfyx grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to install and use ORVIA in the Customer's own environment, for the plan, term and limits shown in the order.
- ORVIA runs in the Customer's environment. **Cyberfyx has no access to it** and receives none of the Customer's operational records, except as clause 5 describes.

## 2. Term, renewal and fees

- The term is shown in the order. It renews only when the Customer pays for a renewal.
- Fees are shown in the order, plus GST. They are paid by UPI or card through Cyberfyx's payment gateway.
- Fees are not refundable except as the law requires, or where Cyberfyx ends the licence under clause 9(b).

## 3. Customer responsibilities

The Customer is responsible for:
- its own compliance with the DPDP Act and Rules and any other law;
- the security of its environment, its user accounts and its backups;
- the data it records in ORVIA.

ORVIA helps with compliance. It does not make the Customer compliant.

## 4. Support and updates

- Support is by the vendor portal and email during [[support hours]].
- Support never needs the Customer's operational data. Diagnostics are prepared by the Customer, reviewed and approved in ORVIA before anything is sent.
- Updates are signed packages that the Customer chooses to install.

## 5. Data

- **Account data:** Cyberfyx processes the name, business contact and sign-in records of the Customer's staff who buy, administer or contact support. See the privacy notice and the data processing agreement.
- **Audit evidence:** ORVIA sends nothing to Cyberfyx unless the Customer, in ORVIA, approves an audit mandate or releases an evidence package for an engagement with Cyberfyx Consulting. That engagement has its own engagement letter.
- **No telemetry:** ORVIA sends no analytics, usage data or crash reports.

- Cyberfyx's total liability in any 12 months is limited to the fees paid in those 12 months. The limit does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of the data processing agreement.

## 9. Ending the licence

- (a) The Customer may stop at the end of any term.
- (b) Either party may end the licence for a material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice.
- On ending, the licence file expires, and ORVIA keeps the Customer's data readable in the Customer's environment for export.
