# Data processing agreement

**Template v1.0.** Draft for management approval; not legal advice. It is written to meet s.8(2) of the DPDP Act, 2023: a Data Fiduciary may engage a Data Processor only under a valid contract. The wording of s.8(2) must be checked against the official gazette text.

**Between:** [[Client legal name]] (the "Data Fiduciary") **and** [[Cyberfyx legal name]] (the "Data Processor").
**Date:** [[date]]. This agreement forms part of the ORVIA subscription terms and of every audit engagement letter between the parties.

## 1. What is processed, and why

| Processing | Data | Data Principals | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Subscription and support | Name, business email, phone, role and sign-in records of Client staff who buy, administer or contact support for ORVIA | Client staff | Licensing, billing, support, security of the vendor portal |
| B. Audit evidence items released by the Client | Only the items a Client approver releases one by one, flagged as containing personal data, under a per-item exception | Client's customers, employees or others, as named in each item | Performing the audit engagement named in the item |
| C. Remote inspection | Records viewed on a Client-operated screen share; nothing is kept | As above | Performing the audit engagement |

Automatic audit evidence under an audit mandate carries no personal data by design. It is outside this agreement except where it is found to contain personal data, in which case clause 7 applies.

## 2. Instructions

- The Processor processes personal data only on the Fiduciary's documented instructions: this agreement, the engagement letter and each release in ORVIA.
- It processes only for the purposes in clause 1.
- It never uses the data to train any model, for analytics, for marketing or for any other client.

## 3. Personnel

Only Cyberfyx staff named on the engagement team in the vendor area may access released items. Each is bound by confidentiality. Each view and download is logged.

## 4. Security

The Processor applies the measures in the **security and breach commitments** annex, including:
- encryption of stored items;
- mandatory multi-factor sign-in;
- row-level access control;
- logging of every access, kept at least one year.

## 5. Sub-processors

- **Hosting provider for the vendor service:** [[name, location]].
- **Payment gateway (processing A only):** Razorpay Software Private Limited, for card and UPI payments. Cyberfyx stores only payment references.
- The Processor gives 30 days' notice of any new sub-processor. The Fiduciary may object, and if the objection is not resolved, may end the affected service.

## 6. Location

Personal data is stored in [[India / named region]]. It is not transferred outside India without the Fiduciary's written consent, and never to a country restricted under s.16.

## 7. Breach

- The Processor tells the Fiduciary of any personal data breach affecting data under this agreement **without delay, and in any case within 24 hours** of becoming aware.
- The notice gives what is known of the nature, extent, timing, likely impact and measures taken.
- The Processor gives the help the Fiduciary needs to meet its duties under s.8(6) and Rule 7, including the 72-hour detailed report to the Board.

## 8. Rights of Data Principals

The Processor passes to the Fiduciary, within 2 working days, any request it receives from a Data Principal. It helps the Fiduciary answer requests under ss.11–14.

## 9. Retention and erasure

- Released items are erased at the end of the retention period in the **retention schedule** annex. The default is 90 days after the engagement closes.
- On request, the Processor confirms erasure in writing. Items under a legal hold are kept until the hold ends, and the Fiduciary is told.

## 10. Audit of the Processor

The Processor gives the Fiduciary the information it reasonably needs to show compliance with this agreement: the vendor-visibility record in ORVIA, access logs for released items, and the retention purge record.

## 11. Term

This agreement lasts as long as any processing in clause 1 continues. Clauses 3, 7 and 9 survive it.
