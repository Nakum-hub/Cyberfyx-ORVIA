## 1. The service

Cyberfyx will perform a **[[Readiness assessment / Evidence audit]]** of the Client's processing of digital personal data against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("the criteria"), version [[criteria version]] with digest [[criteria digest]].

- **Requirements in scope:** Appendix A.
- **Period:** [[from]] to [[to]].
- **Systems in scope:** as listed in the Client's signed scope statement.
- A **readiness assessment** gives findings and recommendations only, and no opinion.
- An **evidence audit** gives an opinion as at a stated date, for the stated scope.
- Neither is a certification. Neither may be described by either party as "certified", "DPDP certified" or similar.

## 2. How the work is done

Cyberfyx follows its DPDPA audit methodology v[[1.0]]. The work uses the ORVIA product the Client runs in its own environment:

1. **Audit mandate.** The Client approves, in ORVIA, an audit mandate naming the requirements, evidence categories, schedule and dates. Two different Client people prepare and approve it. The Client may suspend or revoke it at any time. While it is active:
   - the Client's ORVIA sends personal-data-free evidence, signed by the Client's installation, to Cyberfyx's audit address only;
   - Cyberfyx never connects to, signs in to or reads the Client's systems.
2. **Evidence files and samples** that may contain personal data are sent only when a Client approver releases them item by item, under the data processing agreement.
3. **Remote sessions.** The Client lets Cyberfyx auditors inspect sampled records on a Client-operated screen share. No recordings are made, and no personal data is kept in Cyberfyx working papers.
4. **External tests.** The Client **authorises** Cyberfyx to act as a member of the public using a synthetic identity Cyberfyx controls. Cyberfyx may:
   - read notices and consent banners;
   - give and withdraw consent;
   - file access, correction, grievance and nomination requests to the Client's published channels.

   Cyberfyx tells the Client's DPO the synthetic identity at the end of fieldwork.
5. **Interviews and walkthroughs** by video call.

## 3. The Client's responsibilities

The Client:
- is responsible for its compliance with the criteria;
- provides complete and accurate information;
- signs the scope statement before fieldwork;
- answers auditor requests by their due dates;
- signs the management representation letter before the report is issued.

Requests unanswered by their due date are reported as scope limitations.

## 4. Independence

- The ORVIA subscription fee does not depend on the outcome of this engagement, and neither does the fee for this engagement.
- Cyberfyx confirms that no person who implemented or advised on the Client's ORVIA set-up or DPDPA programme in the last 12 months will review this engagement, or perform procedures on their own work.
- The Client may engage a different auditor at any time.

## 5. Report and use

- **The report is delivered only to the Client.** Cyberfyx does not send it, or any part of it, to the Data Protection Board of India or any other authority, unless the law compels disclosure, in which case Cyberfyx tells the Client first where the law permits.
- This engagement is **not** the audit by an independent data auditor that a Significant Data Fiduciary must have under section 10(2)(b) of the Act and Rule 13 of the Rules. Cyberfyx does not offer that audit.

- The report is addressed to the Client's [[board / management]] for the intended users named in the acceptance record.
- The Client may share the report in full, including every limitation, but not in extracts that change its meaning.
- Cyberfyx may correct an issued report by a signed correction, which the Client's ORVIA imports and shows.
