# Scenarios SC-001 – SC-040 — E-commerce, Retail, Quick Commerce, Food Delivery, Loyalty

End-to-end business scenarios. Each chains several modules and must be run on a fully built, installed ORVIA with fictional organisations and synthetic principals only.
Retention periods, deadlines and fiduciary classes: use the reviewed rule pack loaded in the environment, and record the pack version in the result. All scenarios start **NOT_RUN**.
